# LinkedIn rules

## Limits
Headline 220 chars · About 2,600 (first ~300 show before "see more") · each experience description 2,000 · 50 skills · up to 5 pinned top skills.

## Draft offline, apply in the person's browser
Write every field in `linkedin-profile.md` first. Then apply section by section in the person's own logged-in browser (an extension, not a headless browser: LinkedIn blocks automation and it breaks their terms). The person clicks Save on every form. Keep "Notify network" off on every form, and "Share profile updates with your network" off in Settings → Visibility.

## Staged rollout
If the current employer shouldn't learn about the search, update everything up to the previous role first (titles, dates, bullets, skills, honors), use an interim headline that doesn't announce a search, and hold the current-employer entries, the full About and Open to Work (public) until the person says go. Open to Work set to "Recruiters only" is private.

## Skills
- Rule: one abstract skill covers its specifics (IAM covers SAML/OIDC/Okta; SAST covers Semgrep). Only skills shipped in the last 4 years or owned today.
- Keep verified signals: a passed Skill Assessment, or 5+ endorsements on a skill that's still true.
- Delete legacy stacks even when endorsed; they attract the wrong recruiters.
- Attach 3-5 skills to each role (the "N experiences" line is what recruiters see), and tie skills to certifications where they exist.
- Adding skills via the form: type, pick LinkedIn's suggestion (custom text is allowed but weaker for search), tick the roles, Save.

## Sections to clean
Honors (name the award, issuer, date, one factual line), Publications (only real pieces), Projects (close dates or remove dated ones), Volunteering (real organizations only; GitHub and Stack Overflow are not volunteering), Education (programs go under Licenses & Certifications, degrees stay), contact email (professional domain).

## Headline formula
[Role] · [2-3 expertise keywords] | [credential or signature achievement] | [stack] | [city]. Searchable terms beat slogans.

## About structure
Hook (one line) → who you are with years and domains → 5-6 metric bullets → how you work → stack → education, location, work authorization, call to action with email. Technical detail belongs in experience entries, not About.
