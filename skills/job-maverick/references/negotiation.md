# Compensation research and negotiation

## Research before the first recruiter call
1. **Posted ranges first.** In pay-transparency jurisdictions (BC, Ontario from 2026, several US states) employers must print the range. Collect 8-12 recent postings at the target level; they beat survey averages.
2. Then crowd-sourced sites (levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Indeed) for p25/median/p75, knowing they skew to big tech and self-reporting.
3. Then recruiter guides (Hays, Robert Half) for the conservative bound.
4. Write `compensation.md`: band table by title, local vs remote vs US-remote, a verdict with a **walk-away floor** and a **target anchor**, and bonus/equity norms by company stage.

## The two numbers
- **Floor** (walk-away): never said aloud. Set it at or above the bottom of posted bands at your level.
- **Anchor**: the number you say when asked. Set it near the midpoint of the posted bands for the companies you want, so the settle point lands where you'd be happy.
- A candidate who quotes a floor below the posted minimum signals a lower level and gets anchored there.

## Answering "what are your expectations?"
- Where a range is posted: "I saw the <X-Y> band; given <scope and proof>, I'm targeting the upper half, around <anchor>."
- Where none is posted: give one number anchored on comparable postings, not a range.
- Where salary-history questions are banned (BC, many US states), don't volunteer history.
- Counter once, with one number, 8-15% above the first offer, citing a specific posting or datapoint.
- If base is capped: signing bonus, equity size and refresh, a 6-month review, vacation.

## Beyond base (Canada)
RRSP/DPSP match 3-6% · extended health, dental, vision · vacation 3-4 weeks standard, 4-6 at Staff · parental top-up · learning budget. Options at a CCPC get favourable tax treatment; ask.

## Immigration-linked value
If a provincial nominee program needs a local employer and a qualifying offer, an employer's written commitment to support it is worth real money; ask for it in the offer stage. Remote roles for out-of-province employers may not qualify.

## Founding / early-stage roles
Cash is traded for equity. Accept a base below the floor only with meaningful fully-diluted equity, a visible valuation, disclosed runway, and a path to the floor within 12 months.
