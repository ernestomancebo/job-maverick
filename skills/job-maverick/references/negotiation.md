# Compensation research and negotiation

Market-specific rules (pay-transparency laws, benefits norms, immigration leverage) live in `markets/<market>.md`. This file is the method.

## Research before the first recruiter call
1. **Posted ranges first.** Where employers must print the range, collect 8-12 recent postings at the target level in the target market; they beat survey averages.
2. Then crowd-sourced sites for p25/median/p75 (Glassdoor, Indeed, Payscale; levels.fyi for technology roles; union pay grids for nursing and teaching; association surveys for facilities and finance), knowing each skews.
3. Then recruiter guides (Hays, Robert Half, Randstad) for the conservative bound.
4. Write `compensation.md`: band table by title, local vs remote, a verdict with a **walk-away floor** and a **target anchor**, and bonus/equity/pension norms by employer type.

## The two numbers
- **Floor** (walk-away): never said aloud. Set it at or above the bottom of posted bands at your level.
- **Anchor**: the number you say when asked. Set it near the midpoint of the posted bands for the employers you want, so the settle point lands where you'd be happy.
- A candidate who quotes a floor below the posted minimum signals a lower level and gets anchored there.
- Public-sector and unionized roles (hospitals, school districts, municipalities) pay on published grids; negotiation there is about **step placement** (credit for prior experience), not the band.

## Answering "what are your expectations?"
- Where a range is posted: "I saw the <X-Y> band; given <scope and proof>, I'm targeting the upper half, around <anchor>."
- Where none is posted: give one number anchored on comparable postings, not a range.
- Where salary-history questions are banned, don't volunteer history.
- Counter once, with one number, 8-15% above the first offer, citing a specific posting or datapoint.
- If base is capped: signing bonus, step placement, a 6-month review, vacation, professional-development budget, equity where it exists.

## Beyond base
Pension or retirement match · health, dental, vision · vacation · parental leave · licence and association fees paid · continuing-education budget · shift differentials and overtime rules (healthcare, facilities) · relocation.

## Immigration-linked value
If an immigration program needs a local employer and a qualifying offer, an employer's written commitment to support it is worth real money; ask for it at the offer stage. See the market file.

## Founding / early-stage roles (any profession)
Cash is traded for equity. Accept a base below the floor only with meaningful fully-diluted equity, a visible valuation, disclosed runway, and a path to the floor within 12 months.
