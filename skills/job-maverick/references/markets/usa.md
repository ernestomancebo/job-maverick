# Market: United States

Applies to any profession. Add state specifics as you meet them.

## Resume format
- 1 page for under ~10 years' experience; 2 pages for senior and leadership candidates. The master resume is exempt.
- No photo, age, marital status, or SSN. No "References available on request".
- Header: city and state (no street address), phone, email, LinkedIn; portfolio link where the profession has one. Work authorization only if asked on the form; don't volunteer visa status on the resume unless you're authorized without sponsorship and want to say so.
- ATS-clean: single column, standard headings, no tables or text boxes, ATS-safe characters.
- Summary 2-4 lines; achievement bullets with numbers; reverse chronological; at most 5 bullets per role on tailored versions.

## Pay transparency and negotiation
- Posted ranges are required in California, Colorado, Washington, New York, Illinois, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Maryland, Hawaii and a growing list; use those postings as the comp source even for remote roles.
- Salary-history questions are banned in many states and cities; don't volunteer history.
- Total comp usually includes bonus and equity; ask for the equity grant in dollars and the vesting schedule. Benefits that matter: health plan cost share, 401(k) match (3-6%), PTO (15-25 days), parental leave.
- Employment is at-will; severance is negotiable at senior levels.

## Immigration and work authorization
- Many employers filter on "requires sponsorship now or in the future"; answer the form truthfully. H-1B transfers and TN (Canadian/Mexican professionals in listed occupations) are the common paths; TN is per-role and the job title must match the treaty list.

## Regulated professions
- **Nurses:** NCLEX plus state board licensure; compact (NLC) licences cover member states; CGFNS for foreign-educated nurses.
- **Accountants:** CPA is state-issued; foreign designations map via NASBA/IQEX for some bodies.
- **Teachers:** state certification; reciprocity varies.
- **Engineers:** PE licensure by state, needed for stamping work; most software roles don't require it.
- **Building/facilities:** certifications (CFM, FMP, RPA, CPM) and trade licences for team members.
