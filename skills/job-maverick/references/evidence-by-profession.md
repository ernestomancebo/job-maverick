# Evidence by profession

What counts as proof, where to find it, what the portfolio is, and where the jobs are posted. Use this to write the quiz for phase 1 and the discovery plan for phase 3. Add professions as you meet them.

| Profession | Scope numbers to ask for | Outcome numbers to ask for | Systems of record (where numbers live) | Portfolio / public work | Where jobs are posted (beyond general boards) | Regulated? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## How to use it in the quiz
1. Read the resume. Identify profession and setting.
2. For each role, ask the **scope** numbers first (they are easy and anchor the story), then **outcomes**, then where the number lives (the source).
3. If a number has no source, mark it "to confirm" in the brag doc; it does not go on the resume until confirmed.
4. Ask about **regulation** before targeting: an unlicensed nurse or an uncertified teacher in the target market is a credential project first, a job search second.

## Discovery, honestly
`jobs-scan.py` covers employers that publish on Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever, which skews toward technology companies and startups. For the professions above whose jobs live on health-authority portals, school-district boards or association job lists, say so, search those boards directly, and record findings in `pipeline.json` with `pipeline.py add`.
